# Product Principles

## Our Mission

Home Compass helps families make confident home-buying decisions by reducing the cognitive load of the home-buying process.

We don't simply organize information. We help users understand what matters, what can wait, and what to do next.

---

## Design Principles

### Reduce Cognitive Load

Every feature should reduce the amount of information users must remember, organize, and prioritize themselves.

If a feature increases complexity without reducing mental effort, we should question whether it belongs.

---

### Help Users Decide What to Do Next

Every screen should guide action.

Users shouldn't have to wonder:

- What should I do now?
- What deserves my attention?
- What can wait?

The interface should make those answers clear.

---

### Show Context Before Action

Before asking users to make a decision, provide the information they need.

Example:
Display existing tasks before encouraging users to create a new one.

---

### Separate Importance from Urgency

Priority measures importance.

Urgency measures timing.

These are different concepts and should be represented independently.

The application should help users understand both.

---

### Progressive Disclosure

Surface the most important information first.

Allow users to drill into supporting details without overwhelming them.

Example:

Due Tomorrow

followed by

Tuesday, July 14

instead of forcing users to calculate urgency themselves.

---

### Consistency

Every screen should reinforce the same mental model.

The Dashboard answers:

"What deserves my attention?"

A Home answers:

"What needs to happen for this home?"

Every component should support that distinction.

---

### In One Sentence

Home Compass should think ahead so the user doesn't have to.

OR

Home Compass organizes information so families can focus on making decisions, not managing complexity.
